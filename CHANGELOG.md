# Changelog

What each release of this repository holds, newest first. A release is a tag on `main`,
published on GitHub and archived on Zenodo with its own DOI.

## 1.0.0

Not released yet. The date is written here on the day of the release, which is made just before
the conference paper is submitted.

The first release: the repository as it stands when the conference paper is submitted.

- The shared package `pynmdf`: the readers of the three public sources, the cleaning rule and
  the network total, the three targets, the daily models M2 to M6, the POE levels, the
  rolling-origin backtest and the figures.
- The conference paper, in `publication/01_conference`: its eight notebooks, its committed
  results (figures, tables and checks), its decision record and the archive of the first
  version of its results.
- The first looks behind the paper, in `exploration/01_conference`.
- Tests that need no data files.
